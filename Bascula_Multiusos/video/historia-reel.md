### Clip 1 · 0–5 s · F0 → F1

```
For the first two seconds, Tostabot, the tagged character element, leans toward the
camera and whispers a secret, one wrench hand beside his face, his LED smile moving
softly and slyly as if speaking quietly. Then he straightens up excitedly, turns and
steps onto the glass bathroom scale behind him as the camera pulls back to a
full-body shot. The moment his weight lands, the scale turns translucent like x-ray
glass and four small metal bars glowing electric blue appear in its corners, bending
downward under his weight. His LED smile widens and flexes as he talks, his round
glowing eyes stay on. One continuous shot, no cuts. No text.
```

### Clip 2 · 5–10 s · → F2

```
Tostabot stands on the x-ray scale for a beat while the four glowing blue bars flex
down and up as he shifts his weight. Then he steps off the scale, the glow fading
behind him, and walks toward the camera, which pushes in to a chest-up shot. He asks
a question with an excited tilt of the head, and on the final line points
energetically DOWN with both wrench hands toward the empty lower part of the frame.
His semicircular LED smile flexes as he talks, his eyes stay on. The lower half of
the frame stays empty and softly blurred. One continuous shot, no cuts. No text.
```

### Clip 3 · 10–15 s · F2 → F2

```
Tostabot keeps pointing DOWN with both wrench hands toward the empty lower part of
the frame, bouncing slightly with excitement, nodding once, his big semicircular
LED smile glowing, his round eyes blinking softly once. He does not speak. The
camera stays still. The lower half of the frame stays empty and softly blurred.
The shot ends in exactly the same pose as it began, for a seamless loop. No text.
```

## Edición

1. Audio en 0 s, los 3 clips seguidos desde 0 s.
2. **Sticker del reel (o de enlace) a partir de 8.1 s**, cuando dice "¡Corre a ver
   nuestro nuevo reel!", en la zona que señala Tostabot (≈ 60–78 % de la altura).
3. Si el susurro de 0–1.9 s suena bajo, súbelo +3 a +5 dB solo en ese tramo.
4. Opcional: el texto **"NUEVO REEL 👇"** entre 8 s y 15 s, arriba del sticker.
